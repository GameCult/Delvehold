---
title: Workshop play
description: The daily practice, space, relationships, and responsibilities of one independent workshop.
---

The workshop is a private production shard, a home for useful practice, and the material basis of one civic seal. The player begins by scrying, preparing, crafting, repairing, carrying, and tending processes directly.

Manual work teaches the material rules that later automation expresses. It should be tactile, readable, and brief enough that learning a process is satisfying without requiring permanent repetition. The workshop grows through new practices, tools, spatial choices, assistants, relationships, and connections to shared infrastructure.

Space remains scarce. A workshop chooses what to keep near, what to automate, what to commission, and which commons or neighbors to depend upon. Expansion cannot silently annex another shard or multiply political authority.

The cozy fantasy comes from accumulated familiarity: named stations, remembered materials, recurring customers and delvers, repaired tools, small rituals, and a machine whose history remains visible. Efficiency is useful, but the workshop is not reduced to throughput.
