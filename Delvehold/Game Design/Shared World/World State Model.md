---
title: World state model
description: The canonical subjects, components, events, and derived projections of DELVE/HOLD.
---

DELVE/HOLD canonical state is typed CultCache state published through CultMesh. Components are sparse and owned: an expedition has participants and a contract; a workshop has facilities and a seal; a resource has custody and provenance. There is no universal property bag or client-owned mirror promoted to truth.

## Greathold state families

| Family | Canonical contents |
| --- | --- |
| People and authority | players, delvers, parties, Masters, workshop identities, civic seals, institutions, control and consent |
| Holds and presence | Vaults, Holds, routes, active attachments, presence leases, dormancy receipts, capacity, provenance and jurisdiction |
| Economy | commodity definitions, resource lots, recipes, facilities, inventories, custody, orders, realized trades and price observations |
| Contracts and expeditions | issuers, objectives, kits, rewards, parties, lifecycle, routes, findings, injuries, losses, returns and histories |
| Dungeon ecology | cores, lifecycle pressure, populations, adaptations, memory, topology, hazards, responses and extraction provenance |
| Civic life | assemblies, proposals, seals, testimony, ratified mandates, capacity commitments, withdrawals and institutional actions |
| Information | facts, observations, reports, rumours, sources, confidence, disclosure and knower scope |
| Continuity | world time, revisions, events, commands, receipts, causal references, schema versions, policy versions, generation seeds, generator versions, input and output digests |

## Derived state

Scenes, dashboards, prices, digests, forecasts, generated dungeon rooms, news layouts, active navigation, active Hold populations, local discovery, settlement recommendations, and the aggregate Greathold boundary are projections. They may be rebuilt from canonical state and cannot repair it.

A workshop identity persists independently of its nullable Hold attachment. The identity owns its seal, practice, cold configuration, history, relationships, and custody. The attachment owns current local participation and its versioned 36-hour presence lease. Dormancy removes the attachment through one receipted transition; a later activation may attach the same identity to another Hold.

The world host commits each dungeon generation record and its provenance.
CultGeometry mesh artifacts are deterministic outputs of that record. Godot
resources are disposable adaptations of the artifacts, not another persistence
layer.

Quantities, conservation, recipes, orders, contracts, and production capacity remain Delvehold authority. Ghostlight's current strategic resource handles are not a substitute for the HOLD economic model.
