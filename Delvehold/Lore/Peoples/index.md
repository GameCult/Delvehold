An elven delegation arrives with a model of nested spirits. Goblin guides already know which tunnels have changed their habits. Orcish treaty-keepers produce obligations absent from dwarven maps; surveyors answer with measurements taken at great cost. In human capitals those documents become rival laws, while halfling field books show what reached the crops before any embassy agreed on a name.
