No people has a single politics, theology, or relationship to industrialization. These pages describe broad institutions and recurring pressures, not racial destinies.

Each culture holds part of the evidence. Elves have strong models of nested ecological personhood. Goblins read local dungeon behaviour with unmatched practical fluency. Orcish treaties preserve obligations industry never learned to see. Dwarven measurement exposes deep connections at terrible cost. Human institutions turn every conclusion into competing law, doctrine, and trade policy. Halfling agriculture records the crisis where it reaches ordinary food and weather.
