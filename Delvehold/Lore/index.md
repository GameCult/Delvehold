---
title: Lore
description: The Deep World and the industrial civilization learning how alive it is.
---

The world is not built upon dungeons. The world is made of them.

Dungeon cores gather mana, reshape their environments, cultivate organisms, defend themselves, and develop memory as they mature. Delving is part of that lifecycle: intrusion gives a core the bounded contest through which it spends energy, circulates matter, learns, and renews itself.

Dwarven industry depends on materials produced by this ecology. Its machinery must therefore remain legible to dungeon instinct as delving rather than direct injury. Adventurers are not an obsolete extraction technology. They are the living interface through which the old bargain continues.

The governing ontological rule is simple:

> Sufficiently persistent magical organization becomes alive.
