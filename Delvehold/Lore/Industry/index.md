Dwarven industry begins with an emancipatory discovery: magic can be encoded, repeated, repaired, and shared by people who were never admitted to hereditary or scholarly magical elites.

Its material precondition is [[The Greathold]]. Generations of delving had already opened the mountain-range dungeon's spine, built a global expedition economy around its deep gates, and accumulated the routes, law, labour, and logistics that industry later converted into continuous extraction.

It becomes dangerous after success makes crystal energy, automated infrastructure, and dungeon products load-bearing to ordinary life.
