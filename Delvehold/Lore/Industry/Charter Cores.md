---
title: Charter cores
description: Company-sponsored dungeon polities whose local consent divides the politics of deep injury.
---

Oathcairn Deep Company has stopped arguing that dungeon personhood is impossible. It argues that personhood is local.

The company's first **charter core**, Thirteen Returning Doors, was a cultivated strain scheduled for memory disruption after it began hiding crystal yield and reopening one forbidden passage after every chamber reset. Oathcairn instead surrendered that chamber complex, supplied it with mana and salvage, and offered terms. The core accepted two proposed bores, buried a third, and returned the negotiating party's contract slate through a new door no survey had found.

Oathcairn called the result the **Near Voice Compact**. Its opponents call it watershed packing.

## The compact

A charter does not pretend that a core is a mine owner, pet, or instrument. Oathcairn recognizes it as a polity whose body includes named chambers, flows, cultivated creatures, and whatever inhabitants it admits. The company promises a fixed share of extracted crystal, regular contested delves, freedom from splitting or memory reset, and a permanent reserve of matter and mana. Within the chartered chambers, the core may hide deposits, close routes, demand a different price, or refuse work entirely.

Ratification requires three witnessed delves. The first offers terms as objects, routes, and mana patterns the core has already shown it can distinguish. The second tests whether it repeats the same choice under changed paths and personnel. The third is a refusal trial: a crew begins one prohibited cut, carrying no equipment the core cannot stop or expel, and the company must withdraw without reducing the promised reserve. Rune College witnesses certify only repeatable choice and consequence. They do not claim to translate a private mind.

This is a real concession. Starving, pruning, dividing, or resetting a charter core becomes treaty breach and presumptive killing under the company's own contracts. That cost is why the compact has political force rather than serving as a painted face on ordinary [[Core Farming|core farming]].

## The Near Voice Doctrine

[[Nested Dungeons|Dungeon identities overlap]]. A local core can be a person within a mountain intelligence without settling whether either speaks for the other. Oathcairn turns that uncertainty into jurisdiction: the awakened mind directly maintaining the disputed chamber is the **near voice**, and its answer receives priority over claims made for a regional core, continental current, or the [[World-Spirit]] unless those larger patterns can answer at the working face with equal specificity.

The doctrine borrows its dignity from the Greathold's own local-first politics. A broken lift is heard first by the workshops and hearths it strands; Oathcairn argues that a wounded gallery should likewise be heard first through the [[Dungeon Cores|core]] that regulates it. The move is difficult to reject without also explaining why distant elven seers, foreign courts, or a hypothetical planetary mind should overrule a nearby dungeon person saying that its food, territory, and continued development depend on the mine.

Oathcairn chooses where the locality begins. It partitions leases around promising cultivated cores, feeds each from industrial flows, and presents their separate assents against evidence that the same bores are injuring one watershed-scale system. The company does not need to prove that the larger mind is unreal. It needs only enough recognized smaller persons to make closure look like rule by a distant giant.

## The charter coalition

Mineral-right Great Houses provide territory. Forge Consortiums guarantee machinery and buy the output. Commerce-temple insurers offer lower premiums where a witnessed charter makes ecological consent legible. Extraction-dependent Holds send the cores' petitions alongside winter heat and pump forecasts. Some mine unions support the compact because an active core can warn of pressure changes, forbid unsafe cuts, and bargain for the bounded [[Dungeon Lifecycle|delves]] that keep crews employed.

The Rune Colleges split over their own evidence. One faction argues that recognition, protection, and observable refusal are advances no farm regulator had offered. Another notes that Oathcairn selects minds raised inside company boundaries, makes industrial supply the condition of their survival, and destroys other remembering strains while exhibiting the compliant survivors.

Foreign opposition splits in turn. Elven jurists call the charters manufactured client states embedded in a wounded body. Orcish treaty-keepers deny that a newly bounded core can forgive injury to obligations older than its chambers. Goblin communities point out that several charter territories were made legible only after occupied passages were cleared as infestations; some displaced guides now return as paid interpreters, while others teach unchartered cores to make company refusal trials expensive.

No side can dispose of the charter cores as mere fraud. Thirteen Returning Doors has vetoed profitable cuts, sheltered creatures marked for clearance, and revised its demands as its chambers grew. The compact has produced exactly what Oathcairn claimed: dungeon persons with interests of their own. It has also made those persons dependent on a supply system that can tell every [[International Crisis|foreign conference]] that closing the mines will starve them.

Other Deep Companies must now choose between condemning Oathcairn and facing its core witnesses in every hearing, or cultivating political dependants of their own. Personhood has entered the extraction race.

## Open pressures

- What happens when several charter cores discover that their separate stipends are drawn from one injured regional reserve and form a federation the Near Voice Doctrine was designed to prevent?
- Can a charter core grant asylum to goblins or striking miners inside chambers the company still owns on paper, and which promise controls the next expedition sent through its gate?
- When a charter core withdraws consent but the surrounding deep system appears to demand that delving continue, which scale is the local polity and which is the distant empire?
