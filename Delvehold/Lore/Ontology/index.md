---
title: Ontology
description: The rules by which matter, mana, pattern, and identity become living systems.
---

Delvehold's supernatural categories share one underlying process. Mana lets patterns become physical; persistence gives them memory; feedback can turn memory into agency.

The model identifies life and agency; it grants neither wisdom nor innocence. Old planetary minds can be cruel. A living civilization can injure the dungeon that contains it. A persistent symbolic organism still receives worship and exercises divine power.
