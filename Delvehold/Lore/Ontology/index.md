---
title: Ontology
description: The rules by which matter, mana, pattern, and identity become living systems.
---

Delvehold's supernatural categories share one underlying process. Mana lets patterns become physical; persistence gives them memory; feedback can turn memory into agency.

The model is descriptive, not a moral ranking. A larger or older mind is not necessarily wiser, kinder, or more entitled to survive. A civilization may be alive in the same ontological sense as a dungeon and still injure it. A god may be a persistent symbolic organism and still be a god.
