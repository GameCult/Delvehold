The planet is the oldest and deepest dungeon.

No jewel waits at the geometric centre. The geological core is the densest part of a mind carried through mana currents, fault lines, crystal strata, oceans, volcanoes, ancient dungeon networks, and the life between them.

Different traditions call aspects of it the Earth Mother, Deep Father, Sleeping Queen, World Serpent, Foundation, First Mountain, or Buried God. Soil and season shape rites to the Earth Mother. Foundation cults attend to bearing stone; stories of the Sleeping Queen preserve dreams separated by generations. Each name carries the encounter that produced it.

## Timescales

An earthquake may be a reflex. A cave system can close over decades like scar tissue, while monster migrations and mana storms move through the shorter rhythms of fever or defence. Other changes take longer than the cultures observing them.

No public demonstration joins every event to one cognition. Dwarven deep surveys have found correlations their institutions struggle to explain. Elven and orcish traditions preserve older encounters, much of it excluded from industrial evidence.

## The present danger

The current crisis may be injury propagating through [[Nested Dungeons|nested systems]]. It may mark the moment an ancient organism notices the machine-shaped pattern growing through its upper layers. The first coherent answer could arrive as speech, a defence, or a change too slow for its makers to recognize.
